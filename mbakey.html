    What is Key Club?

    The oldest and largest service program for high school students, and Mater Brickell's home for service, leadership and friendship.

    01
    Our mission

    Key Club is an international, student-led organization that gives members the chance to serve, build character and develop leadership.

    It's part of the Kiwanis family, and every club is sponsored by a local Kiwanis club of adult volunteers. At Mater Brickell, that means cleanups along Biscayne Bay, food drives for our neighbors, tutoring younger students and showing up for our school. Members run the meetings, plan the projects and can run for office at the club, division and district level.

    The Key Club pledge

    I pledge, on my honor, to uphold the Objects of Key Club International; to build my home, school and community; to serve my nation and world; and combat all forces which tend to undermine these institutions.

    i.

    Leadership

    Leading with humility. A real leader listens, recognizes others and lifts them up.

    ii.

    Character

    Everyday kindness, integrity and teamwork that make our community stronger.

    iii.

    Caring

    Compassion is the center of Key Club. It's what gets us out of bed on a Saturday.

    iv.

    Inclusiveness

    Everyone is welcome, whatever their background. Service is better with more people.

    Home / Hours

    Every hour counts.

    How to log your service hours, and how many hours we've given to Miami together this year.

    04
    Log your hours

    Four steps, every time.

    1. Get the event supervisor to sign your hour log before you leave.
    2. Turn in your signed log to the Secretary within two weeks.
    3. Only unpaid service for nonprofits and the community counts.
    4. Check your total with the Secretary before each quarter ends.

    Bright Futures volunteer requirements have been 100 hours for Academic Scholars and 75 for Medallion Scholars. Confirm current numbers with your school counselor.

    Home / FAQ

    Good questions.

    Everything new members usually ask.

    07

    Still curious? Ask any officer, or send us a message.

    Contact us
    Do I need volunteer experience?+

    No. Most members join as freshmen with zero hours. We'll show you how everything works at your first event.

    How many hours do I have to do?+

    The club sets a minimum each year to stay in good standing. Ask the Secretary for this year's number. Most members go well past it.

    Do Key Club hours count for Bright Futures?+

    Service with nonprofits and community organizations generally counts, but your school counselor approves the final hours. Keep your signed logs.

    What's the difference between Key Club and Kiwanis?+

    Kiwanis is the adult service organization that sponsors Key Club. Key Club is the high school branch of the same family.

    Can I run for an officer position?+

    Yes. Members in good standing can run in the spring elections, and Florida also has division and district leadership roles.

    Are there dues?+

    Yes. Dues cover your Key Club International and Florida District membership. Ask the Treasurer for this year's amount.
